# Research notes (verified Nov 2026)

## Exam years
- Year 11 in 2026/27 sit GCSEs in summer 2027; Year 10 in 2026/27 sit in summer 2028.
- Current specifications remain in place for 2027 and 2028. The Curriculum and Assessment Review
  (final report 5 Nov 2025) recommends ~10% less exam time, but that is tied to NEW GCSEs (not before ~2029+).

## Formula / equation sheets
- Ofqual decision 5 May 2026: formulae and equation sheets continue in GCSE Maths, Physics and Combined Science
  for the lifetime of current specs, i.e. 2028 onwards too (AQA news 1 Sep 2026). 2027 sheets = 2026 sheets.
  Boards must publish each year's sheet by 1 Sept of the previous year.

## AQA changes from summer 2027 (AQA news, 21 Sep 2026)
- All subjects (except MFL): fewer question formats (no circling words; fewer draw-lines / letters-in-boxes),
  consistent tick-box format for ALL multiple-choice questions, single left-aligned column for sources with
  line numbers on the left, less bold/italics/capitals/underlining.

## AQA GCSE English Literature 8702 (valid 2027 & 2028)
- Shakespeare (choose 1): Macbeth, Romeo and Juliet, The Tempest, The Merchant of Venice, Much Ado About Nothing, Julius Caesar.
- 19th-century novel: Jekyll and Hyde, A Christmas Carol, Great Expectations, Jane Eyre, Frankenstein, Pride and Prejudice, The Sign of Four.
- Modern texts: An Inspector Calls, Blood Brothers, DNA, A Taste of Honey, Princess & The Hustler, Leave Taking,
  Lord of the Flies, Telling Tales, Animal Farm, Anita and Me, Pigeon English, My Name is Leon.
  REMOVED (last exam 2024): The History Boys, The Curious Incident (play), Never Let Me Go.
- Poetry clusters: Love and Relationships, Power and Conflict, Worlds and Lives (first exam 2025).

## GCSE Maths formulae sheet (2027; same as 2026; continues 2028+)
Foundation & Higher: trapezium area, prism volume, circle C & A, Pythagoras, right-angled trig ratios,
compound interest P(1 + r/100)^n, P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B).
Higher adds: quadratic formula, sine rule, cosine rule, 1/2 ab sin C, P(A and B) = P(A given B) P(B).
NOT given (must learn): rectangle/triangle/parallelogram areas, cuboid/cylinder volume, arc & sector, angle facts,
similarity k, k^2, k^3, exact trig values, circle theorems, y = mx + c & perpendicular gradients, x^2 + y^2 = r^2,
index laws, difference of two squares, speed/density/pressure, % change, proportion, independent events, histograms.
(Sources: Mathinar 2027 sheet article; Cognito Sept 2026 article; AQA news 1 Sep 2026.)
